Retreat Offerings

Nourishment for the whole person

Creative and inspiring retreats — mentally, spiritually, physically, emotionally, relationally.

“…Jesus went out to a solitary place.”

Group Spiritual Direction (2 hr)

Group spiritual direction offers a unique introduction where participants learn what it means to be listened to and loved by others, so that they can learn to listen more attentively to God in their daily lives and be used by God to spread God's grace and love throughout the world.

“Spiritual direction leads you to an awakening of the soul.” — Alice Fryling, Seeking God Together

Immanuel Journaling & Interactive Gratitude (2 hr)

Immanuel means that God is with us. God is always present and desires to interact with us in all situations, though we often forget this truth. Immanuel journaling and interactive gratitude is a simple but profound method for improving our awareness of God's presence in both our thoughts and lives — healing emotional wounds, enhancing character and building community.

— Jim Wilder, Living From the Heart Jesus Gave You

Reflective Retreats (3 hr)

Reflective retreats are not speaker-driven, but emphasize reflective time, providing the participant a wide variety of opportunities to connect with God.

Silence and Solitude Retreats (9am–3pm)

Ongoing one-day retreats designed to give space for rest, renewal and inspiration — a chance to separate from daily activities and responsibilities and free oneself from the addiction to and distraction of noise. This day is dedicated to experiencing God's transforming presence.

“To open oneself to God in the place beyond words.” — Ruth Haley Barton, Invitation to Silence and Solitude

Girlfriend Retreats (3 days)

A group of women committed to journeying together, through a variety of group and individual spiritual formation experiences. These are best done over a 3 day getaway in the mountains or at the beach.

Spiritual Rhythms and the Enneagram

The Enneagram is a personality tool that offers profound insight into our heart motivations — the attitudes and behaviors that affect our personality and relationships. It offers a way of seeing both the beauty of how God made you and the sin, or addictions to self, that mess up your life. Partnering with the Holy Spirit, it becomes an effective way to discover something of the beautiful person we always are in the mind of God, offering paths to transformation in marriage, parenting, work, ministry, and your relationship to God.

— Adele and Doug Calhoun, Spiritual Rhythms and the Enneagram

Note: All retreats can be customized and locations can vary depending on groups and organizations.

A retreat is a time and space set apart to be intentional about one's relationship with God. A retreat is about listening, waiting, receiving, and being. It is not about getting anything done. A retreat is about slowing down enough to be caught by God — a setting where we can experience God's unique and effective care for our soul, and be still and experience ourselves as uniquely loved by God.

— Larry Warner, Journey with Jesus
