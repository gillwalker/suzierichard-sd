Breakthrough and freedom

Inner Healing

Using the tools I've learned in inner healing and trauma, I walk alongside those who feel stuck and desire to experience breakthrough and freedom in their lives.

“Have you ever had the experience of feeling so stuck that nothing seems to breakthrough? Oftentimes, what hinders forward movement is the presence of fear and shame…

One of the many problems with shame is… you cannot talk someone out of it. Shame is the most powerful force in the lives of people…apart from grace. Through the process of inner healing, shame can be dismantled in a way to allow an experience of grace to breakthrough. In our time together, we will explore the use of Inner Healing in various forms of companioning.

This model is grounded in a Trinitarian theology perspective — understanding the heart of God to be relational in nature, inviting, healing, and redeeming. Our starting point for understanding humanity is found in Genesis 1 — being created in the image of God; everyone is endowed with the Imago Dei inside. The use of imagery to uncover hidden aspects of self is a unique distinctive to the process.”

— Imago Dei Ministries
