Welcome

Soul Care and Spiritual Director

I'm passionate about creating safe spaces and opportunities for people to experience God and His healing presence. I specialize in guiding personal and group spiritual formation and soul care experiences for individuals, women's groups and ministry leaders. My sweet spot is walking alongside those who feel stuck in their emotional pain and trauma and allowing the process of God's inner healing work to take place in a way that allows an experience of grace to breakthrough.
